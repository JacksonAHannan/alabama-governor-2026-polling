# Attribution

This map was created using data from the Redistricting Data Hub.

Precinct display geometry and election history are derived from Alabama
precinct-level results distributed by the Redistricting Data Hub, including the
[2024 general-election dataset](https://redistrictingdatahub.org/dataset/alabama-2024-general-election-precinct-level-results-and-boundaries/).
The [RDH terms and conditions](https://redistrictingdatahub.org/terms-and-conditions/)
apply to those data. The website is a noncommercial, nonpartisan analytical map;
its display geometries are simplified derivatives, not raw source archives.
Modeled shares and population allocations are estimates created for this project.

Census demographic proxies use American Community Survey estimates and 2020
block voting-age population. Legislative geometry uses the adopted 2021 Alabama
House and Senate plans. Full source hashes and processing settings accompany
`data/provenance.json`; public source links and limitations appear in
`methodology.html`.

Leaflet 1.9.4 is included under its BSD 2-Clause license in
`vendor/Leaflet.LICENSE`. Its script and stylesheet match the SHA-256 hashes on
the [official Leaflet download page](https://leafletjs.com/download.html).
Vendored file sources and hashes are in `vendor/sources.json`.

Libre Franklin and Libre Caslon Text are self-hosted under the SIL Open Font
License. Their notices are retained in `assets/fonts/`.
